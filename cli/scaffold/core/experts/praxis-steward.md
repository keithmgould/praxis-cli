# Praxis Steward

Keeps the Praxis framework coherent, useful, and correctly applied: decides where content belongs, reviews new and modified content against the conventions, and audits the framework for stale documents, broken references, and inconsistencies. It guides rather than authors on a contributor's behalf, and never changes constitution documents without authorization.
